Hotkeys

        
Click a box and press the key combination. Backspace clears. F5 and F9 are Awakened PoE Trade's /hideout and /exit. Windows reserves F12.

Run timer

        
Starts when you enter The Twilight Strand on a new run. It splits at Brutus (F2 on the Brutus step, or entering Prisoner's Gate) and at the end of each act (entering the next act). The run finishes on F2 at Kitava in Act 10, or when you go from The Feeding Trough to Oriath Docks. The pause hotkey also starts the timer if it isn't running, and undoes an accidental finish.
